0Read this first

If this guide does not talk about it, it works the same as before. This guide only shows the main settings and the new features. Currency, Bonus Betting Multiplier, Applicable Port, Mission Audit Record, Blacklist settings and language settings are the same as before.

What's new

  • Mission Group: a new Mission Category. Put many sub missions on one card. You can also add Extra Rewards.
  • Quota Limit: set how many players can get a reward. When no places are left, players see "Full".
  • Reward types: added Free Spin, Free Bet, Coupon and Golden Draw Ticket.
  • Game Page Display: new name for the old “任务标签” (task tag) field. It works the same.
  • Front-end tabs: "Limited Time" and "Weekly Limited Time" now show in one tab: "Limited Time Task".

Common terms

TermMeaning
PeriodThe time until a mission starts again. Daily = 1 day. Weekly = 1 week. Permanent and Limited Time missions have only 1 period.
ResetA new period starts. Progress goes back to 0, and players can do the mission again. Daily = every day at 00:00. Weekly = every Monday at 00:00.
Greyed outThe field is grey. You can't click it or type in it.
QuotaThe most players who can get a reward.
FullNo places are left. Players who did not get the reward yet can't get it now.

2Create a Basic Mission

1
Basic Info

Choose "Basic Mission" in Mission Category. Type the Mission Name. Players see only 1 line, so a long name ends with "…".
Game Page Display: to also show the mission on a game page (Sports, Slots, Live…), choose that page. If not, choose "None".

2
Mission Content

Mission Condition: first choose the type, then the condition. Recharge and Bet need an amount. It must be a whole number above 0.
Forward Address: the page players go to when they tap "To do", "Top Up" or "Bet Now".
Mission Reward: Bonus, Points, Free Spin, Free Bet, Coupon or Golden Draw Ticket. For Free Spin, Free Bet and Coupon, also type a "Value". Players see it as ₱Value.
Quota Limit: the most players who can get the reward in each period. First come, first served. Blank or 0 = no limit.

3
Participants

VIP Level and User Label: if you tick nothing, everyone can join. If you tick some, players who don't match can't see the mission.
If you tick more than 1 label, players need all of them. Example: tick TH and ML, and players need both.
Requirements for participating in the mission (KYC, total recharge, total valid bet): only work when ticked. Players who don't meet them can still see the mission. The button tells them what to do first.

4
Time & Delivery

Mission Time: Permanent, Daily, Weekly, Limited Time or Weekly Limited Time. Each condition only allows some of them. The others are grey (see the red text).
Audit Method: Auto Audit = the mission is done when the player reaches the goal. Manual Audit = the mission is done only after you approve it in "Mission Audit Record".
Reward Distribution Method: Auto Distribution = the reward goes to the player right away. User Self-Claim = the player must tap "Receive".
Reward Expiry Time: only shows for User Self-Claim with Daily or Limited Time. If you type N days, players can still get missed rewards in "Reward Retrieval" for N days after the reset or end.

5
Others, then click "Submit"

Sort: a smaller number shows higher on the player's page. Missions that are done or Full move to the bottom by themselves.
Display Mission Description: choose "Yes" to show ⓘ next to the title. Players tap it to read the Mission Description.
Mission Status: choose "Turn on" so players can see it.

What players see

3Create a Mission Group

The steps are the same as a Basic Mission, but steps 1 and 2 are a bit different. When you choose "Mission Group", "Game Page Display" goes away, and Mission Content looks like this.

  • Sub Mission: starts with 1. Click "＋ Add sub mission" to add more. You can have up to 10. Each one needs a Name, Condition, Reward and Redirect.
  • Extra Rewards (you can skip this): "Complete any n" gives 1 more reward. It does not matter which ones the player finishes. Up to 3 levels, and not more levels than sub missions. Click "Delete" if you don't need it.
  • Mission Time: the whole group uses 1 Mission Time. So all sub mission conditions must work with the same Mission Time. Conditions that don't work are grey.
  • Quota Limit: each sub mission has its own count, with the same number. Extra Rewards have no limit.

6Key rules to remember

After you click Submit, you can't change: the Mission Category. For a Mission Group, you also can't add or remove sub missions, and you can't change Extra Rewards at all.
  • 2025 new version access, 关注Facebook, 体验平台PWA: only 1 of each in the whole Task Center. Sub missions count too.
  • Christmas Countdown Mission, Brand Daily Passcode: Basic Mission only. Mission Time must be "Limited Time". To limit players, use the "Daily participation limit" field (same as before).
  • Permanent missions: can't have a Quota Limit.
  • Missions with a quota and Extra Rewards: not in Reward Retrieval. Players must get them before the period ends.
  • Coupon "Value": only shown to players. If you leave it empty, players see ₱0.
  • The order on the player's page doesn't change right away: after a player gets a reward, or after you change Sort, players must refresh the page to see the new order.
